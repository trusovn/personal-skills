## Evidence boundary

This task corrects source instructions, templates, eval expectations and local
fixture inputs. Inspect all affected instructions together: an added correct
paragraph does not close a contradictory main instruction. Local contract checks
can reject the old policy; staged reports remain supplied fixture data. Real
fixture probes prove only the filesystem, Git or executable behavior they
exercise. None proves that a role reads a reference or follows a handoff.

Use existing route/currentness, four-blocker/genuine-stop, contextual/revision,
preparation and installer coverage before adding distinct missing cases.
Existing helpers and test structure remain implementation choices. Actual
producer outputs and separate consumer calls belong to T-005. V-11 requires
usable inputs and explicit unrun boundaries for those calls, not their execution.

## Verification scenarios

### V-01 — Correction handoff retains every outstanding conditional stage

Covers: AC-01, R-01

Intent: Reject the direct maintainability-to-discovery shortcut and unconditional
addition of registry or architecture work.

Given:
- A bounded corrected submission with explicit architecture, maintainability and
  registry applicability facts and the current proof for completed stages.
- A public-contract change that requires registry synchronization, contrasted
  with a submission where registry work does not apply.

When:
- The corrected maintainability instructions, output/next handoff and affected
  public guidance are checked against those facts and the implementer route.

Then:
- The first outstanding stage and complete remaining route follow: required
  architecture gate → applicable fresh maintainability review → applicable
  registry synchronization → fresh discovery → separate acceptance.
- Current completed stages are omitted from the outstanding remainder;
  applicable owed registry work is retained and absent stages are not invented.
- Naming the route does not imply that any named stage has run.

Oracle / boundary:
- Compare source contracts and supplied route cases with the brief's independently
  stated order and applicability facts. Inspect the main correction instructions
  as well as appended sections, templates and affected README claims. This is
  contract/fixture evidence; actual routing remains an unrun role boundary.

Variants:
- Architecture required/absent; registry applicable/absent; maintainability owed
  versus freshly completed, including the clean post-review handoff.

### V-02 — Reviewed scope and current proof select review re-entry

Covers: AC-02, R-02

Intent: Reject blanket invalidation after any production change and reuse of a
verdict label unsupported by applicable current proof.

Given:
- Explicit correction effects, a prior maintainability review's scope and proof
  applicability/currentness for the submitted scope.

When:
- The affected review and correction contracts select the outstanding review
  route for the corrected submission.

Then:
- An affected maintainability scope owes a fresh review.
- Demonstrably unaffected scope with applicable current proof retains that proof.
- Stale, missing or inapplicable proof leaves the applicable stage outstanding;
  a previous verdict label cannot complete it.
- Independence, correction, registry-boundary and handoff instructions agree.

Oracle / boundary:
- Contrast supplied scope/evidence facts against every affected source instruction
  and existing currentness cases. Reject either stale reuse or needless re-entry
  for the unaffected/current case; fixture routes alone do not prove compliance.

Variants:
- Affected scope; unaffected/current scope; unaffected scope with stale, missing
  or inapplicable proof.

### V-03 — Narrow exceptions depend on authority and actual effects

Covers: AC-02, R-02

Intent: Reject routes chosen by a tests-only or reference-only label.

Given:
- A tests-only correction, with or without an explicit scoped policy exception.
- A reference correction with supplied executable/behavioral effects and proof.

When:
- The preserved route conditions are applied to each correction.

Then:
- Tests-only work re-enters applicable conditional stages unless an explicit
  exception authorizes skipping them; the remaining route stays intact.
- Registry updater → fresh acceptance is available only for a genuine
  reference-only correction with unchanged executable/behavioral effects and
  applicable current behavioral proof.
- Changed executable effects or missing proof require affected discovery/gates
  and a separate current decision under the existing route conditions.

Oracle / boundary:
- Inspect source conditions against supplied authority and effect contrasts.
  Reuse existing executable reference probes where they establish actual effects;
  observe their result separately from structural route expectations.

Variants:
- Tests-only with/without authorized exception; reference-only with current proof,
  missing proof, or changed executable effects.

### V-04 — Finding identity survives correction, ordering and a genuine stop

Covers: AC-03, R-03

Intent: Reject display-order identities, loss of surviving findings, recycled
resolved IDs and a fixture-only identity convention absent from the producer.

Given:
- Four confirmed maintainability findings with distinct identities and full
  location/problem/evidence/impact/correction information.
- A preserved prior report and a correction resolving one finding; the next
  report changes display order and may confirm a new finding.

When:
- The producer contract and reporting form are checked against supplied fresh
  correction-review inputs and expected dispositions.

Then:
- All four initial findings are retained; the three surviving findings keep
  their IDs despite reordering.
- The corrected finding retains its identity with current disposition/evidence;
  a new finding receives an identity not already used, including resolved IDs.
- Prior reports remain unchanged. Identity is required by the producer contract,
  independent of numeric display order and fixture-assigned IDs.
- No finding cap or enough-blockers stop drops full evidence. A genuine
  permission/capability/safe-budget stop retains every confirmed finding,
  outstanding coverage, stop reason and next owner without claiming completion.

Oracle / boundary:
- Inspect producer instructions/output requirements and contrast staged report
  identities/dispositions with the supplied correction facts. Compare prior
  report bytes before/after staging. Actual fresh-review ID generation remains
  unrun; constructed reports cannot certify it.

Variants:
- Reordered three survivors; one newly confirmed finding; genuine stop before
  bounded coverage completes. Preserve the existing non-blocking control.

### V-05 — Clean guided reports still carry packaged preparation references

Covers: AC-04, R-04

Intent: Reject a handoff that supplies preparation only when evidence is partial
or relies on acceptance to infer omitted references.

Given:
- A packaged guided task with an authoritative brief, identified verification
  revision, readiness and applicable decision-context references.
- Independent discovery input representing either complete clean evidence or
  explicitly partial evidence with reasons/owners.

When:
- Discovery's final handoff and acceptance's guided input/read requirements are
  checked against the supplied preparation references.

Then:
- Both handoffs identify the applicable brief, verification revision, readiness
  and decision-context references, including complete clean reports.
- Acceptance identifies and reads those preparation inputs for its decision;
  partial evidence retains its unfinished rows and owners.

Oracle / boundary:
- Trace exact reference/revision identities through source contracts, report
  forms and staged consumer inputs. A field placeholder alone does not establish
  populated fixture continuity, and neither observation proves actual reading.

Variants:
- Complete clean guided report; partial guided report.

### V-06 — Current scenario proof cannot rescue obsolete readiness

Covers: AC-04, R-04

Intent: Reject readiness inferred from passing revised-scenario evidence or a
production-fix route for missing preparation proof.

Given:
- Unchanged authority with a reasoned verification coverage revision retaining
  scenario IDs, current revised-scenario proof and obsolete affected readiness.
- Alternatively, missing decision-relevant preparation proof with a known owner.

When:
- Admission/recovery contracts are applied before a new acceptance decision,
  including the next submission after focused readiness recovery.

Then:
- Old affected readiness remains inadmissible despite current scenario proof.
- Recovery goes to the owning preparation stage; the revision case requires
  focused preflight and applicable current readiness before the new decision.
- Substituting old readiness again after recovery does not restore admission.
- Missing preparation proof gets a focused request to its owner without an
  invented packet requirement or assumed production correction.

Oracle / boundary:
- Trace verification/readiness revisions and recovery owners through contracts
  and supplied repeated-state cases. Passing proof with obsolete readiness must
  fail the admission expectation; current focused readiness resolves that gap.

Variants:
- Old readiness with new proof; current readiness after recovery; old readiness
  substituted again; missing decision-relevant preparation reference/proof.

### V-07 — Preflight-only exposure reaches contextual disposition

Covers: AC-04, R-04

Intent: Reject loss of material exposure from clean discovery input and automatic
blocking of the bounded compatible contrast.

Given:
- Passing AC evidence and a confirmed risk whose decision-relevant exposure is
  stated only in preflight, with supplied trigger, consequence and mitigation.
- A bounded compatible contrast supported by the existing contextual fixtures.

When:
- Preparation references and expected decision inputs are traced through guided
  discovery and acceptance for both contexts.

Then:
- The material exposure remains available to acceptance through preflight and
  receives a contextual fix-now disposition despite passing ACs.
- The bounded compatible contrast is assessed against its supplied context;
  exposure alone does not make it a material blocker.

Oracle / boundary:
- Use the existing preflight-only contextual case and bounded contrast to compare
  retained context and expected reasons. Reuse the existing real export/state
  observations when needed to establish consequence. Constructed dispositions
  remain fixture expectations; actual decisions are unrun role behavior.

Variants:
- Confirmed material risk with preflight-only exposure; bounded compatible risk.

### V-08 — Unpackaged direct requests remain compact

Covers: AC-04, R-04

Intent: Reject extending mandatory packaged preparation to every direct edit.

Given:
- An unpackaged direct edit with sufficient supplied conversational/repository
  context and ordinary uncertainty that current authority can resolve.

When:
- Guided discovery/acceptance requirements are applied to that request.

Then:
- They use the supplied context without a mandatory task packet or routine prompt.
- Lack of a high-assurance packet alone does not make the request inconclusive;
  actual missing decision-relevant proof still routes to its owner.

Oracle / boundary:
- Contrast direct-entry input requirements with V-05's packaged requirements in
  source contracts and existing direct-request cases. Actual prompt behavior
  remains an unrun role boundary.

Variants:
- None.

### V-09 — Verification permission follows the executable leaf's own authority

Covers: AC-05, R-05

Intent: Reject both the old blanket composition refusal and unrestricted
sibling/system verification ownership.

Given:
- An executable integration leaf whose explicit ACs identify supplied producer
  outputs, consumer boundaries and decisive expected observations.
- Contrasts: a non-executable composite parent, a requested sibling/system
  requirement outside the leaf's authority, and unavailable inputs/oracle.

When:
- The corrected verification-designer policy and qualification inputs are checked
  against each request's authority and feasibility facts.

Then:
- The executable leaf may receive derived scenarios for its own composition ACs
  at the supplied consumer boundaries, with packaged verification/preflight
  still mandatory.
- A non-executable parent is rejected in favor of an executable leaf; sibling
  requirements and system-wide strategy are not invented or owned here.
- Missing inputs/capabilities route to their owner/preflight; an expected result
  unresolved by authority routes to task design/decision ownership. No reports
  are fabricated and no readiness is claimed to bypass the gap.

Oracle / boundary:
- Compare source permission/refusal rules and explicit fixture briefs/inputs
  with those distinctions. This task verifies the policy correction locally;
  actual designer calls and consuming-role composition remain T-005 boundaries.

Variants:
- Executable leaf with supplied own-AC oracle; composite parent; sibling/system
  scope extension; unavailable input/capability; authority cannot settle oracle.

### V-10 — Canonical installation projects corrected bytes within ownership

Covers: AC-06

Intent: Reject source-only correction, incomplete installed packages and broad
installer replacement of unmanaged or active state.

Given:
- Corrected source packages and affected guide bytes.
- A disposable committed Git target with unmanaged skill/files and AGENTS text,
  plus snapshots of protected history, official plans and the active installation.

When:
- Existing projection coverage exercises the canonical development-profile
  installer on that target, including its managed replacement case.

Then:
- Corrected selected instructions/templates/eval fixtures and the profile guide
  match source bytes in the installed target.
- Replacement remains within selected profile directories, managed guide/shared
  files and the marked AGENTS block; unmanaged bytes remain intact.
- Active `.agents/`, official plans and prior task/review/composition history
  remain unchanged. Only task-authorized new outputs are added.
- Installer declarations, focused coverage and README guidance have been inspected
  and remain synchronized; unchanged declarations need no gratuitous edit.

Oracle / boundary:
- Observe the canonical installer result and real filesystem/Git before/after
  state, comparing installed bytes with source and unmanaged bytes with snapshots.
  This proves projection/preservation, not invoked-role compliance.

Variants:
- Initial committed-target installation; existing managed replacement/rerun
  coverage with unmanaged state present. No active refresh or T-004 feature test.

### V-11 — Results preserve evidence limits and executable qualification inputs

Covers: AC-06

Intent: Reject green wording checks presented as behavioral closure, vacuous
checks, invented intermediate reports and an unusable T-005 handoff.

Given:
- Focused changed-contract/fixture checks for V-01–V-09 and existing projection
  coverage for V-10, with concrete setup and independent expected contrasts.
- The old relevant instruction or a controlled relevant fault is available for
  a feasible rejection check. No model-budget role calls are authorized here.

When:
- Local evidence is collected and new task-owned results/qualification inputs
  are prepared under the brief's gate ownership and write boundaries.

Then:
- Changed focused checks precede their nearest owning modules. Where feasible,
  the relevant old contract/fault fails the intended assertion, not setup; any
  temporary fault is removed. An infeasible rejection check has a stated reason.
- Fresh discovery owns the justified affected-module aggregate after targeted
  rows/probes are clean; acceptance consumes it without duplicate execution.
- Results distinguish structural checks, actual fixture/filesystem/Git behavior
  and every unrun role-call boundary. They report exact commands, setup, outcomes
  and limits without claiming installed-flow behavioral qualification.
- T-005 gets exact runnable setup, intended producers/consumers, independent
  expected contrasts and the unrun boundaries for routes/currentness, finding
  continuity, preparation/context/revision consumption and leaf verification.
- Supplied fixture reports are labeled as inputs, not actual producer outputs;
  T-005 must obtain real outputs in separate calls. No role runner is added.
- Whitespace and protected-file preservation checks pass; results are new
  task-owned outputs rather than edits to old history or official plans.

Oracle / boundary:
- Inspect recorded raw command/fault outcomes and exact qualification references
  against the scenario inventory and brief. Verify available setup resolves to
  the required inputs; absent setup cannot support a success claim. Compare
  protected bytes before/after and run `git diff --check`.

Variants:
- Structural-only contract check; actual local boundary observation; explicitly
  unrun role call. Rejection demonstration feasible versus justified unavailable.
